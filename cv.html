03 / The booking flow

From request to confirmed.

A process-first approach to collecting the right details, confirming status and keeping communication consistent.

Step 1

Request

A client or partner sends a reservation request. I collect the details that matter before anything else moves.

Step 2

Availability & rates

I contact the hotel directly to check availability and rates, and compare options to secure the best one for the client.

Step 3

Confirm

Once the hotel agrees, I arrange and confirm the room booking, and pass the confirmation on promptly and clearly.

Step 4

Record

Every booking is written down accurately, so its details and status are easy to see at a glance.

Step 5

Follow up

I keep following booking status with hotels and companies, and answer inquiries quickly and professionally.
